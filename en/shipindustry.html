Ship Repair & Retrofit

Bringing China's Proven Ship Repair Capabilities to European Shipowners

Through the shipyard, engineering and supply-chain resources of W.Y. Group, FEX and their strategic partners in China, EUREKA Germany promotes Chinese ship repair and retrofit services to European shipowners and ship-management companies. EUREKA Germany handles European market development, commercial communication and project coordination; the China-based team manages shipyard selection, on-site execution and delivery.

Dry-docking, voyage and emergency repairs, ship conversion, and energy-efficiency and emission-reduction retrofits.

Scope of Services

  • Voyage repair at berth or anchorage
  • Main and auxiliary engine repairs
  • Deck machinery inspection and repair
  • Electrical and automation troubleshooting
  • Navigation and communication equipment services
  • Lifesaving and firefighting equipment inspections
  • Steel and piping works
  • Underwater inspection and repair coordination
  • Class and statutory survey support
  • Emergency technical-service coordination

For temporary repairs, technical failures, inspections and emergency services at European ports, EUREKA Germany coordinates local shipyards, riding squads, inspection companies and specialist engineers according to the vessel's schedule, location and technical requirements.

Equipment, Spare Parts & Stores

Connecting China's Manufacturing Capabilities with European Vessel Requirements

EUREKA Germany works with Chinese marine equipment manufacturers, the FEX supply chain and European partners to supply marine equipment, spare parts, consumables and related technical support — coordinating enquiries, technical clarification, supplier selection, quality documentation, consolidation, transport, customs clearance and ship-side delivery.

Engine Spare Parts
Main and auxiliary engine parts, pumps, valves and piping components.
Deck & Electrical
Deck machinery, lifting equipment, electrical, automation and navigation components.
Safety & Consumables
Lifesaving, firefighting, marine lighting, tools, bearings, seals and OEM alternatives.
Provisions & Port Supply

Reliable and Timely Port-Supply Services for Vessels

EUREKA Germany integrates local European resources, the FEX supply chain in China and Wrist's global port-service network to provide ship stores, provisions, bonded goods, spare parts and logistics services.

Ship Stores
Deck and engine-room stores, tools, consumables and cleaning materials.
Provisions
Fresh and frozen food, meat, vegetables, fruit, beverages and dry goods.
Bonded & Crew Supplies
Bonded goods, crew personal items and port supplies per local regulations.
Logistics & Delivery
Procurement, consolidation, warehousing, customs clearance and door-to-vessel services.
Strategic Partnership

Global Ship-Supply Cooperation with Wrist

W.Y. (Europe) Holding GmbH, EUREKA (Germany) GmbH, FEX Shipping & Supply Co., Ltd. and Wrist Ship Supply A/S have entered into a four-party strategic cooperation agreement, cooperating globally in ship stores, provisions, bonded goods, spare parts, port logistics and related maritime services.

EUREKA Germany's Role
  • Develop and maintain European and international customers
  • Collect requirements and coordinate commercial communication
  • Organise enquiry, quotation and order follow-up
  • Connect W.Y. Group, FEX, Wrist and end customers
Wrist's Contribution
  • Global port-supply network
  • Physical supply of stores and provisions
  • Bonded goods and crew supplies
  • Spare-parts and warehousing capabilities
  • Port logistics and ship-side execution across global ports
