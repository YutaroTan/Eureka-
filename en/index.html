About Eureka (Germany) GmbH

Based in Germany, Connecting China and Europe

EUREKA (Germany) GmbH, headquartered in Hamburg, is a joint venture and European business execution platform of W.Y. Group. The company is responsible for European market development, customer relationship management, commercial coordination and project follow-up.

EUREKA Germany promotes competitive Chinese capabilities in ship repair, retrofit, marine equipment, spare parts and provisions to the European market — while coordinating shipyards, riding squads, inspection companies and logistics resources across major European ports.
