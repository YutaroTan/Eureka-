EUREKA Germany — Europe's Gateway to FEX Global

EUREKA (Germany) GmbH is the operating entity for W.Y. Group's German and European cross-border e-commerce operations — covering marine products, industrial goods, green technologies and selected lifestyle products, and connecting the FEX Global platform with European suppliers and logistics partners.

FEX Global (Future Efficiency X(Max)), headquartered in Hong Kong, is a global port-based B2B service and trade platform that launched its Platform 1.0 in May 2026. Built around global port nodes, it connects shipowners, port service providers, marine suppliers, traders, logistics partners and industrial resources into one efficient, transparent and trusted network — spanning 100+ countries and regions, 300+ port nodes and 10,000+ enterprise users.
