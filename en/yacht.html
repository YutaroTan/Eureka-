Core Business

Excellence in Modern Yacht Design & Build

EUREKA Yachts, part of Eureka Marine, designs and manufactures premium yachts and leisure vessels, providing a complete lifecycle solution from planning and engineering to EU assembly, CE/class certification and after-sales support.

Our production footprint spans Croatia (Zagreb, Zadar) and China (Dalian, Qingdao) — with mass production in China and EU assembly/certification in Croatia.

We combine European craftsmanship with innovative propulsion options — including electric, hybrid and solar (hydrogen project-based) — to meet modern performance and sustainability goals.
